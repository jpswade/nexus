# Compact live status board

The HDMI keeps all checks visible beside a priority warning or rotating briefing.
The clock and UK threat assessment occupy one header; local weather uses a compact
panel below the board. Source names/check timestamps are removed from status tiles.
The four original check tiles still open their source/history detail on click.
Briefing cards retain full evidence and source links through Details.

Checks: emergency alerts, electricity bulletins, weather warnings, local floods,
UK internet reports (seven-day window), separate NOAA G/R/S observations, today's
NCSC items, official briefings (seven-day window), camera availability and check
coverage. Coverage is a count of current check results, not a risk level. Grid and
internet report counts do not assert a local outage. An empty cyber feed does not
mean there is no cyber risk. Unknown and stale checks never become a clear state.

The LCD keeps nine compact checks above its existing paginated briefing: the three
space-weather scales share one cell. It retains the coloured threat header, single
clock, countdown bar, camera eye and full network strip. Confirmed Pi-offline mode
still takes over both displays. No Wary-derived household ratings are calculated.
